Ripple by Triggercloud

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 6, 2026  ·  Triggercloud AB, Stockholm, Sweden

1. Introduction

Triggercloud AB ("we", "us", "our") operates Ripple, a TikTok campaign management platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data when you use our Service. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Swedish data protection law.

2. Data controller

The data controller for personal data processed through Ripple is Triggercloud AB, Stockholm, Sweden. For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at hello@triggercloud.se.

3. Data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: Name, email address, and professional role of agency users invited to the platform.
  • TikTok account data: For creators who authorize the Service via TikTok OAuth, we collect TikTok user ID, display name, profile information, and OAuth access tokens.
  • TikTok analytics data: Video performance metrics, audience demographics, retention data, and impression source data retrieved via TikTok's API on behalf of authorized creators.
  • Usage data: Log data, IP addresses, and browser information collected automatically when you access the Service.

4. How we use your data

We use collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing and operating the Ripple platform for authorized agency users
  • Retrieving and displaying TikTok analytics data for campaign management purposes
  • Generating campaign performance reports for agency clients
  • Communicating with users about the Service
  • Ensuring the security and integrity of the platform

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. TikTok data and creator consent

Analytics data is retrieved from TikTok only for creators who have explicitly authorized access via TikTok's OAuth authentication flow. Creators may revoke this authorization at any time through their TikTok account settings. Upon revocation, we will cease fetching new data and will delete stored analytics data for that creator upon request.

6. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data on the following legal bases under GDPR:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide the Service to agency users
  • Consent: Processing of TikTok creator data based on explicit OAuth authorization
  • Legitimate interests: Processing for platform security and abuse prevention

7. Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Service. Agency user account data is retained for the duration of the account's active status. TikTok analytics data is retained for up to 24 months from collection. Upon account termination or deletion request, personal data is deleted within 30 days.

8. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. TikTok OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and accessed only by authorized system processes.

9. Data sharing

We share personal data only with the following categories of recipients:

  • Infrastructure providers: Supabase (database and hosting), Vercel (application hosting) — acting as data processors under appropriate agreements
  • TikTok: API requests are made to TikTok's servers to retrieve authorized analytics data
  • Legal authorities: When required by applicable law or court order

10. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@triggercloud.se. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Cookies

The Service uses session cookies solely for authentication purposes. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

12. International transfers

Some of our infrastructure providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active users of material changes via email. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

14. Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@triggercloud.se.
